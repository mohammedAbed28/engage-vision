## Information processed

When a user requests a post preview, the service processes the image, caption, selected content style and optional planned posting time that the user deliberately submits. The app also receives ordinary technical network information required to provide and secure the service.

## Purpose

Submitted content is processed to generate the requested post outlook and improvement suggestions, maintain service reliability, prevent abuse and investigate technical failures. If the user separately chooses an optional caption revision, the original caption, selected tone and goal, and approved writing directions are processed by the configured AI provider. If the user chooses an image revision, the selected image and only the user-approved edit direction are processed. Revisions are evaluated again by EngageVision. The app does not publish content to social networks and does not require access to a social-media account.
